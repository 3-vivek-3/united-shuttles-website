The Delft Student Badminton Association United Shuttles, formerly known as DSBA USSR (United Shuttles Smashing Right), was founded in the Summer of 2006. What began as a shared love for badminton has grown into a vibrant and welcoming community where students from all backgrounds come together to play, compete, and have fun.

There is always something happening at United Shuttles. Throughout the year, our members take part in competitions during the autumn and spring seasons, challenging players and teams from other associations. We also organise our own events, including an internal tournament and the TU Delft Open, bringing plenty of competitive spirit and exciting matches to the court. And because United Shuttles is about more than just badminton, we regularly organise social events where members can relax, meet new people, and enjoy time together off the court. 

Our goal is simple: to bring badminton enthusiasts in Delft together and create a community where everyone can enjoy the sport. Whether you are looking to compete, improve your game, meet fellow players, or simply have a great time on court, United Shuttles is the place to play, connect, and be part of the badminton community in Delft.
